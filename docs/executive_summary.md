# Flowdesk paid acquisition: which channels pay back, and where the next $50K goes

**Answer.** Put the next $50K into paid search ($30K), affiliate ($15K) and paid social ($10K), and take $5K out of review-site bidding. In the base case that buys about 58 new customers worth $225K lifetime gross margin, for $61K of marketing plus sales cost. Net value is $163K, 19% more than spreading the money pro rata to today's mix ($137K). The recommended plan still wins in the conservative scenario ($37K vs $23K).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paid search | **Scale** | $633 / $870 | 5.2x (4.5-6.0) | 3.4x | Biggest channel (600 customers). Platform over-claims only 1.11x. Payback about 10 months fully loaded at the margin. |

| Review site | **Cut** self-serve bidding | $1,099 / $1,442 | 2.3x (1.2-3.7) | 1.1x | 1.8% signup-to-paid. Self-serve-only LTV:CAC is 0.3x (CI 0.1-0.8). 62% of its customers needed a sales rep. Churn is 1.7x other paid channels (p = 0.01). |

Base assumptions: gross margin 80%, churn excluding the 2025 dunning outage, 60-month LTV cap, sales cost $1,543 per sales-assisted customer, CAC elasticity 0.5. All of these are levers in the Excel model.

## What we found
1. **Proven: review site is not a top channel.** It brings signups (3,089) but only 50 paying customers in matured cohorts. The 19 self-serve customers cost $2,893 each and are worth $937.
2. **Proven: platform dashboards overstate.** Platforms claim 1,482 paid conversions; the database has 1,160 (+28%). Paid social over-claims at a steady 1.73x in every year, so the gap is a definition (1-day view-through), not a tracking break. Platform revenue is 1.6-2.7x the actual first-year cash. Using platform numbers, paid social's CAC looks like $381; the real figure is $659.
3. **Proven: 2025 CAC rose 36-86% in every paid channel because conversion fell everywhere, organic included** (self-serve conversion from about 11% to 6-7%). This is the Project 1 new-business conversion drop, not a channel problem. Fixing conversion is worth more than any reallocation.
4. **Proven direction, assumed size: sales-assisted economics win.** LTV is $9.3K vs $2.5K, and churn is 1.7% vs 3.3% a month, in every channel and size band. The sales cost (not in the data) would have to exceed about $6.8K per deal to erase the gap. Our estimate is $1.5K-3.7K.
5. **Proven: cash payback is fast** (1-4 months on marketing CAC) because 32-70% of customers prepay annually.

## Caveats
- **Proven data defect:** every coupon invoice in this extract is 100% discounted ($0 collected, 3,598 paid invoices). The Project 1 extract of the same invoices shows a median 20% discount. Base case uses the data as delivered. Re-pricing raises LTV 10-25% (review site most), and the ranking does not change.
- Gross margin and sales cost are assumptions. Review-site figures rest on 50 customers (see the confidence intervals). Diminishing returns can't be estimated, because spend never varied enough.
